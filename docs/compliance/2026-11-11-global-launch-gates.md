## Per-market evidence required before enabling distribution

An unreviewed market must remain **BLOCKED**. For each store/country or region retain an evidence record for: legal entity and physical business address; privacy/controller and DPO identity; consumer cancellation and refund rights; product category/licensing and finance marketing; app/age/content constraints; local language and accessibility; tax/invoicing and currency; local privacy/retention and data-subject rights; registration/local representative requirements; cross-border transfer mechanism; processors and international subprocessors; sanctions/export controls; store privacy labels and financial declarations; digital subscription terms and trial transparency; real signed-device/checkout testing; qualified local legal approval and sign-off date.

## Cross-market blockers

- **Philippines:** verify PIC identity, applicable NPC registration/DPO obligations, processing register, data-subject response operations, third-party agreements and breach response (NPC IRR: https://privacy.gov.ph/implementing-rules-regulations-data-privacy-act-2012/).
- **European Union / EEA:** determine GDPR Article 3 targeting and appropriate lawful bases, Article 13 identity and information, rights, Article 27 EU representative if applicable, DPIA as needed, and international transfer safeguards (https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/governance-and-sustainability/digital-and-data-compliance/data-protection-gdpr/index_en.htm). DPO contact does **not** replace the EU representative.
- **UK:** independently assess UK GDPR, UK representative where applicable, UK transfer instruments and consumer subscriptions.
- **Other intended jurisdictions:** assess US state privacy and subscriptions, Canada, Brazil, Australia, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, India, mainland China and every other target country individually with qualified advisers; do not claim a single worldwide rule.
- **Data handling:** build verified provider/processor inventory (Vercel, Supabase, NasrinAI AI/voice providers, Apple/Google, market data and third-party imagery), data flow map, data residency, retention/deletion by data category, backups and processor erasure confirmation. Document privacy rights, identity verification, deadlines, breach notification and contact operations.
- **Security:** native local financial data currently may live in browser storage and Capacitor Preferences without application-level encryption. Require device-bound storage migration, threat model, no-data-loss tests, plus sign-out, backup restore, multi-device deletion and active session revocation. Test the exact signed store builds.
- **Billing:** verify store sandbox purchases/refund/renewal/restore, store catalog and currency/tax setup, consumer trial disclosures, Apple Privacy reports, Google Play Data Safety and Financial Features declarations and subscription cancellation paths.
- **Production:** PR #274 ledger/erasure/retention migrations are not present in MOVO production migration history. Apply only after staging verification and rollback planning. Supabase advisor reported 15 authenticated security-definer functions and leaked-password-protection warning; each must be evaluated individually, with no bulk destructive changes.

No store selection, production database, paid entitlement, app listing or live user record was modified by this documentation change. Compliance approval remains dependent on actual proof and jurisdictional legal review.
