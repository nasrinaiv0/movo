# 2026-11-12 — "Save 20%" on the Annual plan

## Request
Owner: "add 20% save if they subscribe to annual plan".

## Change
- `src/lib/storeProducts.js`:
  - `ANNUAL_SAVING_PERCENT = 20`, the owner's annual pricing (annual = monthly × 12 × 0.8).
  - `annualSavingPercent(pairs)` works the figure out from the store's own monthly and annual prices whenever both
    are loaded. It falls back to 20% when no store prices are known (the website), and returns 0 when the store
    prices show no saving, in which case no badge is shown.
- `src/components/PlanOffer.js`: the Annual toggle shows "Save {n}%". Before, it showed "−N%" only in the apps, and
  only once store prices were loaded. The existing "(save N%)" note under the price still uses the store's prices.
- `src/i18n/catalog/ui-2026-11-12-annual-saving.js`: "Save {n}%" in 11 languages (machine-assisted; review before
  commercial multilingual release). Locales were regenerated with `npm run i18n`.
- `src/App.css`: the badge stays on one line. The Monthly/Annual toggle grows to fit it, up to the panel's width, instead
  of a fixed 240px.
- `docs/commercial/STORE_BILLING_SETUP.md`: the owner must price annual plans at 80% of twelve months in both stores.

## Why the figure follows the store when it can
Prices must come only from the App Store and Google Play. A hard-coded "20%" would be wrong if a store price differed,
and Apple and Google both require price claims to be accurate. So in the apps the badge always matches what the store
charges, and it reads 20% when the stores are priced as the owner set.

## Validation
- `src/lib/storeProducts.test.js`:
  - 20% when no prices are known;
  - 20% from prices set at 80%;
  - the real 17% from other prices;
  - 0 when there is no saving.
- `src/components/PlanOffer.test.js`:
  - the website shows "Save 20%";
  - the app with store prices shows "Save 17%" (its test prices).
- Jest 220 suites, `tsc --noEmit`, strict build.
- Browser (Playwright): no overflow or wrapping of the toggle at 430px (English, default text) and at the Largest
  text size at 375px (German, Filipino) and 360px (English).

## Data and security
None. Presentation only; purchases, entitlements and server checks are unchanged.

## Remaining
