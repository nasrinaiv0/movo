Help Center

Quick answers about using MOVO's personal finance workspace.

How do I add a transaction?

Open Home or Transactions and use the add transaction action. Enter the description, amount, type, category, date, and account, then save. Review the record after saving.

Where are my records stored?

Local-only records are kept in this browser and are not automatically available on other devices. Cloud records require sign-in and a correctly configured MOVO database. Local browser storage is not encrypted by MOVO, so do not use it as a secure vault.

Does MOVO connect to my bank or move money?

No. MOVO currently tracks records you enter or import. It does not automatically read private Apple Pay history, block a card, initiate a payment, or verify that a transaction occurred.

How does NasrinAI use my financial information?

NasrinAI chat requires sign-in and an explicit consent step before the limited financial context described in the interface is sent. Do not submit bank passwords, card PINs, CVVs, or one-time passcodes. AI responses can be inaccurate and are not professional financial advice.

Are push and email notifications active?

The current settings store your notification preferences, but push and email delivery are not connected in this build. The app should not be treated as a source of urgent alerts.

How do I export my data?

Use the Export transactions (CSV) action in Profile when available. Open the exported file carefully because it can contain sensitive financial details. Keep it in a secure location.

How do I clear or delete data?

The Clear Local Data setting removes local browser preferences and local records handled by that control. It does not automatically delete cloud records or third-party copies. Use the official privacy/support contact channel provided by the service operator for cloud-data requests.

Policies

Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the service terms and data-handling information.

If something does not work, note the screen, action, and non-sensitive error message. Do not include passwords, API keys, card security codes, or private financial records in a support report.
