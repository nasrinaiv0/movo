3. Local mode and cloud mode

Local and offline operation uses local storage, IndexedDB and device storage for accounts, transactions, working copies, queued changes, attachments, QR images and preferences. Records stay on that device or browser profile until removed, storage is cleared, or the device removes them. Unsynced records can be lost if storage or encryption keys are lost.

MOVO applies AES-256-GCM to supported financial read caches, local chat-history caches and cached verified-plan data. The main local finance records, offline working copies, sync outbox, attachments, QR images and some preferences are not all protected by that encryption. This is not end-to-end encryption: authorized server and AI processing can read the data needed for the requested feature. An unlocked or compromised device or active same-origin code can expose local information.

Custom card-sticker artwork is processed on the device. Signed-in users’ supported custom stickers, including image data, may also be saved in account preferences and downloaded on other devices. Removing a local copy does not necessarily remove an account copy. You can manage individual stickers in Card Style or request account-data deletion.

When you sign in and use cloud features, MOVO uses its configured authentication and database services. Database policies and server-side ownership checks are intended to restrict records to the authenticated account. Do not use cloud features on a shared device unless you can sign out and protect the device afterward.

6. Retention and deletion

Local copies remain until cleared or removed on that device. Cloud records, account preferences and chat history generally remain while your account is active or until you use a supported deletion function. Some standard Archive entries become eligible for cleanup after 90 days; cleanup currently runs when MOVO opens, so removal is not guaranteed on day 90. Archived loans and receivables have different handling and can remain until permanently removed or the account is deleted.

Clear records resets supported device records and preferences and, with Cloud Sync on, removes supported cloud transactions, financial accounts, budgets, chat conversations, preferences and account QR files. It does not close your account or remove every cloud feature: loans, receivables, bills, goals, automations, Friends records and receipt attachments may remain. Use Settings → Security & Privacy → Delete Account for an account-deletion request.

Verified account deletion removes the cloud account and associated data subject to lawful, limited retention and unresolved purchase processing. It does not retract exports, shared copies or local data on another device. Backups and restricted security, billing or legal records follow the purpose and retention requirements that apply to them. Ask dpo@nasrinai.com about retention, access, correction, export, objection or deletion. We verify requests without asking for passwords or full card details.

18. Referral codes and download links

Each signed-in MOVO account has a short referral code, for example M78038. Your code is part of your Refer MOVO download links and QR codes. It identifies the account that shared the link and contains no name, email address or financial information.

When someone opens your Android link, Google Play receives the code as part of the store address and keeps it as the app’s install referrer. On its first launch, the MOVO Android app asks Google Play for that referrer once, on the device, and keeps only a valid referral code, which is filled in so the person can add it after signing in. On iPhone, the download page shows the code and copies it to the clipboard only when its button is tapped.

A referral code counts only after the person signs in and the MOVO service accepts it. MOVO then records which account referred which, and any referral points, with both accounts. Referral codes are not used for advertising.

MOVO uses Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity to confirm that the official app was used for the referral. MOVO records the linked accounts, verification result, confirmed store payment or refund status, and points earned or used. The referral process does not collect your payment card details.

19. AI receipt scanning and categorization

When you choose Analyze with AI on a receipt photo, MOVO sends that selected photo, or extracted text from a PDF receipt, to the MOVO server and its configured AI provider (OpenRouter and the underlying model provider) to read merchant, date, item, amount, tax and payment fields and suggest categories. MOVO requests that its AI provider not collect prompts for training, but provider handling, cross-border processing and retention remain subject to applicable service terms and laws. Do not include passwords, PINs, card verification codes, one-time passcodes or unrelated people's information in receipts.

AI receipt scanning requires sign-in, qualifying access and a separate in-app confirmation for each receipt. You can decline and instead use basic on-device text recognition, or discard the scan without creating a financial transaction. MOVO does not store the scanned photo or full source text in its AI proxy or provider request logs. When you select Log, the details you approve become a MOVO transaction under your selected account, which may sync to your cloud account under your chosen storage settings. The provider may process the submitted receipt as needed to return the result; cancelling or deleting the MOVO review does not guarantee removal from third-party systems.

If the receipt and selected payment account use different currencies, MOVO downloads general exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API and calculates an estimate on your device. The rate request carries no receipt, amount, account, or sign-in data. A bank or card statement may show a different amount. If you log the transaction, MOVO saves the original receipt total and the conversion or statement amount with the transaction.

AI-extracted fields and categories can be wrong. MOVO shows a summary for review, allows correction, and does not choose your bank, card, e-wallet or cash account on your behalf. VAT included in a total, cash tendered and change are not separate transactions. You remain responsible for checking the logged amount, currency, date, merchant, categories, payment account and any duplicates before relying on reports.

20. Private friend invites

Friends is optional. To create or accept an invite, sign in and choose the display name you want to share. Anyone who has an active invite link and a verified MOVO account can see the name attached to that invite. Invite links carry a random code and expire after 24 hours or after one person accepts. Creating a replacement or revoking the active invite disables the previous link.

MOVO stores a hashed invite code, account identifiers, chosen display names, usernames, requests and connection timestamps to manage Friends. Verified members can search usernames that their owners choose to make discoverable. The search shows only usernames. Recipients choose whether to accept connection requests.

When you preview and approve a monthly tracking summary, MOVO stores the selected snapshot for the friend you chose. The sections you select may include your note, income, expenses, savings balance, weekly net cash flow, financial planning totals, budget and plan progress, and spending category percentages. Friends cannot open your underlying transactions, receipts or accounts. You can revoke access, and removing a friend removes any shared snapshots for that connection.

MOVO saves your uploaded profile photo privately on the account when you are signed in and makes it available to you on your devices. Guests keep their photo on the current device. Search results and shared summaries do not include your photo, email or contacts. Account deletion removes the account’s photo and Friends records.

If you block or report someone, MOVO stores the accounts involved and the type of report for safety and support review. A report does not copy the contents of a financial summary into the report record.

When you choose Scan QR code, MOVO reads camera frames or a selected QR photo on your device. These images are not uploaded or stored by Friends. Only a validated MOVO invitation code is sent for review; scanning does not accept the invite automatically. You can instead paste an invite link. A profile QR image you choose to share contains your chosen display name, initial and temporary invitation code, without your photo, contact details or financial records. Recipients and sharing apps may keep a copy; revoking the invite disables its code, not those copies.
