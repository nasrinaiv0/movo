1. Who is responsible for your data

The MOVO service team operates MOVO and is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@nasrinai.com. This policy describes how MOVO handles information and should be read with applicable privacy laws, including the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012 where it applies.

2. Information MOVO may handle

  • Account information: email address and, if provided, display name and profile details returned by your sign-in provider.
  • Financial records: account labels and types, institution names, opening balances, transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, notes, and other details you manually enter or import.
  • Preferences and artwork: theme, accent color, display choices, notification read-state or settings, card-style selections, and custom card-sticker artwork saved in browser storage. When you are signed in, preferences (but not sticker artwork or scenery photos) are also saved to your account so they follow you across devices.
  • Phone automations: payment and bank-notification details that shortcuts you set up send to MOVO (amount, merchant, card or account), and records of automations that could not be logged until you log or archive them.
  • Voice chat: when you talk with NasrinAI, short audio clips are sent for transcription and spoken replies are generated; MOVO does not store the audio.
  • NasrinAI chat: your message, conversation identifier, assistant reply, and—only after you affirm the relevant consent—the limited financial context needed for the request.
  • Technical information: basic request and error metadata needed to operate and protect the service. MOVO's chat error logging is designed to avoid recording prompts, financial records, or secret values.

Please do not submit passwords, card PINs, CVVs, one-time passcodes, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Store purchase information:

When you subscribe through Apple or Google, the store processes your payment. MOVO receives product identifiers, transaction and purchase-verification data, subscription status, and expiry information to verify and provide your entitlement. Google purchase tokens are used transiently for verification; MOVO stores a one-way hash. MOVO does not receive or store your full payment card number or store-account password.

You can request account deletion in MOVO or at movo.nasrinai.com/account-deletion.html. Deleting your MOVO account does not automatically cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google; cancel it in your store account to stop renewal.

3. Local mode and cloud mode

In local mode, manually entered financial records and accounts are stored in this browser's local storage and persist across page reloads in the same browser profile. They are not synced to other devices and can be removed by clearing browser data or using Clear All Data. Depending on the installed version and completed data migration, MOVO may use application-level encryption for selected locally stored financial and QR records. Older versions and other local content, preferences, exports and artwork may not use that protection. Anyone with access to an unlocked or compromised device or browser profile may still be able to access locally stored information.

Custom card-sticker artwork is processed in your browser and saved locally on this device, including when you are signed in. It is not sent to MOVO's cloud database. You can remove individual stickers from Card Style or remove all local sticker artwork with Clear All Data. Browser storage can be unavailable or full; MOVO will show an error when it cannot confirm that a sticker was saved or removed.

When you sign in and use cloud features, MOVO uses its configured authentication and database services. Database policies and server-side ownership checks are intended to restrict records to the authenticated account. Do not use cloud features on a shared device unless you can sign out and protect the device afterward.

4. How information is used

  • Provide dashboards, transaction lists, account summaries, search, filters, imports, and exports that you request.
  • Authenticate you, protect account boundaries, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain service reliability.
  • Save your selected preferences and notification read state.
  • Generate a NasrinAI response when you actively send a message and affirm the financial-context consent.
  • Respond to valid legal requests and exercise or defend legal claims where permitted by law.

MOVO does not sell your personal financial records. We do not use your records for unrelated marketing purposes in the current product behavior described here.

5. When information is shared

MOVO uses service providers for hosting, authentication, and cloud storage, including Vercel and Supabase where configured. Those providers may process technical or account data to provide their services under their own terms and contractual arrangements.

When you affirm the NasrinAI consent and send a chat message, MOVO sends your message and a limited, account-scoped snapshot of relevant account labels, recent transactions, and budgets to NasrinAI. NasrinAI and any providers it uses may process submitted content under their applicable policies. You can use MOVO without granting this chat-specific consent.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to address security incidents, or to protect rights and safety. Service providers may process data in locations outside your country; the exact location can depend on provider configuration.

6. Retention and deletion

Local browser preferences, manually entered financial records, manually created accounts, and custom sticker artwork are stored in this browser until cleared. This storage is device-specific. Supported updated app versions encrypt selected financial records, but not every kind of local file, artwork, export or preference; older versions may not use application-level encryption. Clear All Data removes preferences, local finance records and custom sticker artwork; individual stickers can also be removed from Card Style. Things you delete in MOVO stay in the Archive for 90 days so you can restore them, and are then deleted. Cloud records and chat history remain while your account is active. Deleting your account in Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your cloud records; it does not remove data stored in a browser, which you can clear with Clear All Data.

To request access, correction, export, blocking, or deletion of cloud data, email support@nasrinai.com from the account email when possible. We may ask for reasonable verification. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Clearing browser storage does not delete cloud records, and signing out does not itself delete them.

7. Security

MOVO uses authentication, server-side access checks, user-scoped database policies, HTTPS deployment, and secret separation as part of its security design. No internet service or storage method is risk-free, and application-level encryption of local records depends on the installed app version, successful key migration and the data category. Keep your device and the email or sign-in account you use for MOVO secure, and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

10. Why we process information and the applicable basis

We use the information you provide to deliver requested tracking, authentication, cloud sync, transaction logging, support and exports; to maintain service integrity and prevent misuse; and to meet legal obligations where applicable. Depending on the processing and the law that applies, these activities may rely on your request or agreement, legitimate interests that do not override your rights, legal obligations or your consent where required.

Financial-context sharing with NasrinAI is optional and requires a separate in-app choice. You may decline it and continue using non-AI MOVO features. Withdrawing consent limits future optional sharing; it does not automatically retract material already processed by a third-party provider. Contact support@nasrinai.com for privacy requests.

11. Service providers and external requests

Cloud-enabled functions use infrastructure and authentication providers, including Vercel and Supabase, which may process the account, financial and technical records needed to operate the service. When you request NasrinAI features, your message and permitted user-scoped context may be processed by NasrinAI and the AI or voice providers used to deliver that feature. Store-purchase verification may involve Apple or Google.

Market prices, currency reference data, icon/favicon services and linked third-party content can involve requests to external services. Depending on the feature and how it is loaded, those services may receive network information such as an IP address, browser or app request metadata. MOVO does not need your bank password or card security code to maintain manually entered transaction records.

Providers may process data outside the Philippines. Appropriate safeguards and applicable contractual or legal protections must be evaluated for international transfers; the operator must keep its processor list and transfer assessment current.

12. Device permissions, receipts and voice

Camera or photo access is used only for optional scanning, attachment or visual features when permitted by your device. The app may process some images locally and may save receipt attachments in owner-restricted cloud storage if you explicitly attach them. Uploaded receipts can contain sensitive merchant, account or personal information, so review images before attaching.

Microphone access is used for voice features when you activate them. Short voice clips may be sent to transcription or voice-response providers to fulfil the request. Permission can be withdrawn through device settings, although the voice feature may then stop working. Do not assume third-party provider retention practices are identical to MOVO's local storage.

13. Security and account separation

MOVO uses account authentication, database access controls and transport-security measures for applicable cloud services. These are designed to reduce unauthorized access, but no internet or device storage can be guaranteed completely secure. A local app lock is a convenience control and does not itself encrypt records or replace server authentication. Finance-vault encryption, where available, is a separate safeguard.

Only provide information necessary for the function you choose. Protect your phone and account access, sign out of shared devices and avoid putting passwords, PINs, CVVs or one-time passcodes in chat prompts, receipts or support requests. Security incidents and legally notifiable breaches are handled according to applicable law.

14. How long data stays and how deletion works

Local-only data remains on the current device or browser profile until you clear it, delete app/browser storage or the device removes it. Signed-in cloud records, chat history and preferences remain while your account is active unless you delete them or request a supported removal. Certain items moved into MOVO's Archive are retained for up to 90 days before scheduled removal; this Archive period does not apply to every type of deleted record.

Use Settings → Delete Account in MOVO or the public account-deletion page to request removal of your cloud account and associated personal data. Local data on another device may need separate clearing. Some data may remain temporarily in backups, security logs or records that must lawfully be retained for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal claims; such exceptions should be limited to applicable purposes and retention requirements. Deleting MOVO does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription.

Backup, incident and legally retained records may have different retention periods depending on the underlying service and legal obligations. This notice does not promise immediate removal from every backup or third-party system; contact our privacy team to ask about a particular record.
