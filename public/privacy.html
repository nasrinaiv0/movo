Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy describes how MOVO (“MOVO,” “we,” “us,” or “the service”) may handle information when you use its personal finance tracking features. The actual information available to us depends on whether you use local-only mode, sign in, enable cloud sync, or choose an optional AI feature.

Your financial information is sensitive. Do not enter bank passwords, card PINs, CVVs, one-time passcodes, or other authentication secrets into MOVO or an AI conversation.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account information: email address, display name, authentication identifiers, and session/security metadata when you sign in.
  • Financial records you provide: account labels, transaction amounts and dates, merchant names, categories, budgets, notes, and imported CSV data.
  • Preferences: theme, display options, notification preferences, and other settings.
  • Support and diagnostics: messages you send to support and limited technical information needed to diagnose errors, protect the service, and maintain reliability.
  • AI requests: the prompt and only the bounded context described in the consent flow, if you explicitly choose to send that context to NasrinAI.

2. Where information is stored

Local-only data may be stored in your browser's local storage on that device. It is not automatically available on your other devices and may be erased by clearing browser data, using private browsing, or resetting the device. Browser local storage is not encrypted by MOVO and should not be treated as a secure vault.

If you sign in and enable cloud-backed features, relevant records may be stored in MOVO's configured database and protected by authentication and row-level access controls. Cloud availability and retention depend on the deployed configuration. Do not assume that switching modes automatically migrates or deletes existing data.

3. How information is used

  • Provide account access, save and display records, calculate summaries, and sync data when enabled.
  • Remember your preferences and provide settings you request.
  • Prevent abuse, secure accounts, troubleshoot failures, and maintain the service.
  • Provide AI assistance only when you initiate that feature and accept the applicable context-sharing consent.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests where required.

4. AI and third-party services

If you use NasrinAI and approve context sharing, MOVO may send your message and a limited set of relevant financial details to NasrinAI to generate a response. The feature should disclose the context being shared before sending it. Avoid including unnecessary identifying information. AI providers may process submitted content to deliver the request under their applicable terms and privacy practices. MOVO does not promise that AI output is correct or that a third-party provider's retention practices are identical to MOVO's.

Authentication, hosting, database, and monitoring providers may process data on the service's behalf. The operator should maintain an accurate list of active providers and contractual safeguards for the live deployment. We do not sell personal information as a business model. Information may be disclosed where necessary to protect users, enforce terms, comply with law, or complete a business transfer subject to applicable protections.

5. Sharing and access controls

Your financial records should be accessible only to your authenticated account through user-scoped queries and database access policies. Security controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and account credentials secure, sign out on shared devices, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access through the official support channel.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records generally remain in the browser until you delete them or browser/device storage is cleared. Cloud records may remain while your account is active and for a limited period in backups, security records, or as required by law. A “clear local data” control affects local browser data only unless it clearly says otherwise; it must not be assumed to delete cloud records. To request access, correction, export, or deletion of cloud-held personal data, use the official privacy/support contact channel provided by the service operator. Some records may need to be retained for security, legal, or legitimate operational reasons.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to, or request a copy of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You can manage local data and preferences through available app controls. Optional AI context sharing should be controlled by an explicit consent step. If you are in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and its implementing rules may apply; rights and remedies depend on the facts and applicable law.

8. Children

MOVO is designed as a general personal finance tool and is not intended for children to use without the permissions required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the service operator through its official channel.

9. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the configured service, including authentication and access controls. No system can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in locations different from yours, subject to applicable legal requirements and safeguards.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may change as features and providers change. The effective date above identifies the current published version. Material changes should be highlighted in the product when appropriate. For privacy requests or questions, use the official support or privacy contact channel provided by the service operator.

This policy is a product-specific starting point, not legal advice. The operator must verify actual data flows, vendors, retention periods, contact details, and applicable legal obligations before treating it as final.
