MOVO · ACCOUNT & PRIVACY

Delete your MOVO account

You can request deletion even if MOVO is no longer installed on your device. Sign in to the MOVO website using the same account you use in the app, then open Settings → Security & Privacy → Delete Account and confirm the request.

Open MOVO to sign in and delete your account

What deletion covers

When your deletion request is verified, MOVO deletes your cloud account and associated financial records, chat records, preferences, receipt attachments, and account QR files, subject to records that must be retained by law or for legitimate security and legal claims.

Local-only records on a device or browser are not uploaded to MOVO and must be cleared on that device using Clear All Data or by removing the app/browser data.

Need help signing in?

For privacy and deletion inquiries, contact the Data Protection Officer, Nasrin Abubakar, at dpo@nasrinai.com. Do not email passwords or payment credentials.

If you cannot access your account, email support@nasrinai.com from the email address associated with your MOVO account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Store subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. Deleting your MOVO account does not automatically cancel a subscription; cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings to prevent future renewals.
