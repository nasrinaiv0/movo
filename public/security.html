A transparent security promise

MOVO uses multiple protections, but no service is risk-free. We do not claim that all local records are currently encrypted, that the system is certified, or that a lost device can always recover unsynced data. Those distinctions matter.

Not a bank. Not a money-moving app.

MOVO does not need your bank password, card PIN, or CVV. It does not access your online banking independently, move money between accounts, or execute trades. A recorded transfer represents your own tracking entry.

02 / SECURITY

Layers of protection, explained simply

Signed-in account separation

MOVO's cloud records use authentication and PostgreSQL Row-Level Security policies to limit user access. Account-scoped server checks add protection; no control eliminates every risk.

Protected native sign-in credentials

Applicable iOS credentials are stored using Apple Keychain, and Android credentials use Keystore-backed secure storage. This is different from encrypting every saved transaction.

Browser and network safeguards

The MOVO website uses HTTPS deployment and configured security headers, including frame protection, HSTS, and a Content Security Policy.

Optional app lock

App Lock and supported biometric unlock can discourage casual access. They do not replace account authentication or independently encrypt local finance records.

Important: offline financial-data encryption

As of 13 November 2026, the production MOVO app does not yet encrypt all local financial records with MOVO-managed application-layer encryption. People with access to an unlocked device or browser profile may be able to inspect locally saved records.

Proposed PRs #289 and #290 add encryption for offline read caches and main local finance/sync records. These changes are still in review and require native-device migration testing before release. We will update this guide and our legal disclosures when verified changes ship.

05 / QUESTIONS

Answers without the technical jargon

Can MOVO move money out of my bank account?

No. MOVO tracks information that you provide. It does not execute bank transfers or independently connect to your online banking.

Does MOVO ask for my banking login, PIN, or CVV?

No. Never enter banking passwords, PINs, CVVs, or one-time passcodes into MOVO fields, notes, or chat.

Can another MOVO customer view my cloud records?

Account authentication and database access policies are designed to prevent cross-user access. Like any online service, MOVO cannot guarantee that security incidents are impossible.

Are all financial records encrypted in the current release?

No. Native sign-in credentials use protected device storage, but MOVO-managed application-layer encryption for the main offline finance records is still under review and not yet deployed.

Must I use NasrinAI or share my financial details with AI?

No. Financial-context sharing is optional and requires a separate in-app choice. External AI processing may occur if you grant consent and request the feature.

What if I lose my phone or delete MOVO?

Eligible cloud-synced records may be available after signing in again. Local-only or unsynced records can be lost. Keep backups securely, and review sync status before clearing a device.

Does deleting my MOVO account cancel my subscription?

No. Mobile subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. Cancel the renewal separately in your store account.

How do I request deletion or report a privacy concern?

Open Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account, use the account-deletion instructions, or email support@nasrinai.com. Please do not send passwords, PINs, CVVs, or one-time codes.

Security is an ongoing responsibility
