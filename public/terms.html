1. What MOVO does

MOVO is a personal finance tracking tool that lets you record, organize, review, and export financial information you choose to provide. Unless a screen explicitly confirms otherwise, displayed balances and totals are calculations from the records available in MOVO and are not verified bank balances.

MOVO is not a bank, payment processor, credit provider, accounting firm, tax adviser, investment adviser, or emergency service. It does not connect to your bank or move money. Optional phone automations that you set up yourself can send MOVO the details of a payment or bank notification so it can be recorded.

Store subscriptions and billing

Subscriptions purchased through the MOVO iOS or Android app are billed and managed by Apple or Google. The store shows the price, billing period, applicable taxes, and renewal terms before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel through your Apple or Google account before the renewal date. You can restore eligible purchases from the same store account while signed in to the MOVO account originally linked to the purchase. Deleting your MOVO account does not cancel a store subscription.

3. Local data, cloud accounts, and exports

Local mode stores manually entered financial records and accounts in this browser's local storage. They persist across page reloads in the same browser profile, but they are not synced to another device and can be removed by clearing browser data or using Clear All Data. Browser storage is not encrypted by MOVO itself. Keep a separate copy of important records.

Custom card-sticker artwork is cropped in your browser and saved in browser storage on this device. MOVO does not send that artwork to its cloud database or sync it to another device. Clear All Data removes custom sticker artwork as well as local financial records.

When cloud features are available and you sign in, records are stored in MOVO's configured cloud database and access is restricted by account-scoped authorization controls. Keep your own records where needed. CSV exports are generated from the records currently available to your session and may contain sensitive financial information; store and share them carefully.
